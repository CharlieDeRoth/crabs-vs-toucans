## Full description (4000)
The toucans are flying for the border, and only one thing stands in their way: crabs. Lots of crabs.

Crabs vs Toucans is a cosy pixel-art tower defence game. Place crabs along the path, upgrade them, and pop every toucan before it gets through.

CRABS WITH CHARACTER
- 6 crabs, each with their own weapon held right in their claws
- 5 upgrades per crab, and at level 3 you choose one of two paths, each with a new look
- Unlock the Golden Crab statue that powers up every crab around it

3 ISLANDS, 3 BOSSES
- Toucan Isles, Toucan City and Toucan Olympus, each with 3 tracks and its own music
- Face Toucanzilla, the cyber Toucanbot and the mighty Toucangod
- Earn up to 3 stars on every match

HEROES
- Recruit the Crab Conqueror, Sir Crabycorn and Shrimp with Crab Coins
- Level them up during a match and aim their special abilities
- Unlock skill trees and hero skins

ALWAYS SOMETHING TO DO
- Classic mode on Easy, Normal or Hard, plus Endless
- A new daily challenge every day and a 7-day login reward streak
- Achievements, records and a crab wardrobe of shell colours

PLAY YOUR WAY
- Works offline, no ads, no tracking
- Colour-blind aid, large text and reduced motion options
- Fits phones and tablets in any orientation

## Age rating questionnaire, suggested answers
Answer these honestly in each store's questionnaire. These are suggestions based on the game's content.
- Cartoon or fantasy violence: **Infrequent/Mild**. Crabs "pop" toucans, which burst into pixels. No blood or death.
- Realistic violence: None.
- Weapons: crabs hold cartoon pistols, a sniper rifle and a minigun. App Store has no separate weapons question, but Google Play's IARC questionnaire may ask. Answer "cartoon weapons".
- Sexual content, profanity, drugs, gambling, horror: None.
- User-generated content, chat, purchases, ads, location: None.
- Likely result: App Store 9+, Google Play "Everyone" / PEGI 7. The stores decide the final rating from your answers.
