Crabs vs Toucans: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Crabs vs Toucans is a game by LobsterGames. We built it to collect as little as possible, and in fact it collects nothing about you.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases. It does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information.

What is stored on your device

Your game progress (unlocked islands, stars, Crab Coins, heroes, skins, records) and your settings (volume, accessibility options) are saved only on your own device. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Backup codes

The Settings screen can create a backup code of your progress. The code is made on your device and only goes where you choose to copy or save it.

Internet

The game works fully offline and does not connect to any server.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information, it is suitable for players of all ages from a privacy point of view.

Changes

If a future version ever changes what is collected (for example adding online leaderboards), this page will be updated before that version is released.
